  confirm run_analysis, or only explored the file. The result comes from `content/people/nomination/rank_in_sandbox.py`
  through `analysis:run`, checked 71/71 against the generator's answer key.
- With several people's reasons in one section the model gave one person a neighbour's reason, hence one section per request.
- The Fly image has no unzip, poppler or soffice: Word, Excel and PDF are not indexed there.
